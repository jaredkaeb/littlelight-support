Privacy Policy

Little Light

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Little Light is a bedtime Bible storybook for families. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information we collect

None that leaves your device. Little Light has no user accounts, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no server backend that stores your information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, using Apple's standard on-device data store:

  • Stories you've bookmarked.
  • Your nightly reading streak.
  • Family notes you write inside a story.
  • Prayer additions you write inside a prayer step.
  • Your selected reading preferences (e.g., reading-level tier).

None of this is transmitted to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, this data is removed with it.

iCloud

If you have iCloud Backup enabled in iOS Settings, Apple may include the app's local data in your encrypted device backup. That backup is between you and Apple — Little Light has no access to it.

Children's privacy

Little Light is designed for families, including children. Because the app does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-party services

The app does not embed third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or tracking SDKs.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
