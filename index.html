Frequently asked

Does Little Light need an account?

No. Open the app and start reading. Your saved stories, streak, and family notes all live on your device.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

None. The app stays out of the way so the story can be the thing.

What ages is it for?

Designed for kids ages 4–10, but the readings work just as well for older kids and adults.

Which translation does it use?

Each story includes a King James Version (KJV) reference you can read in full inside the app.

How does the streak work?

Finish a story's full routine on a given evening and that day lights up. Miss a night and the streak resets — but the lamp re-lights the next time you show up.

I lost my saved stories / streak.

Saved data is stored on-device. If you deleted the app or restored to a new device without an iCloud restore, the data won't carry over. Reach out by email and we can help.

Can I use it on both iPhone and iPad?

Yes — the app is built for both, and the layout adapts to whichever you're using.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything Little Light stores. There is no server-side account to delete because there is no server-side account.
